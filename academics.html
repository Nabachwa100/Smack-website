Our Academic Programs

SMACK follows the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) curriculum, offering both Ordinary Level (O-Level) and Advanced Level (A-Level) programs.

O-Level Subjects (S1–S4)

CategorySubjects
Core SubjectsEnglish Language, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History, Christian Religious Education
Optional SubjectsAgriculture, Fine Art, French, Literature in English, Luganda, Computer Studies, Commerce, Entrepreneurship

House System

Every student belongs to one of four houses that foster brotherhood and healthy competition:

HouseNamed After
Mugwanya HouseStanislaus Mugwanya, prominent Buganda Catholic chief
Kiwanuka HouseJoseph Kiwanuka, first African Catholic Bishop of Kampala
Kakooza HouseHonored Catholic leader in Uganda's history
Lourdel HouseFr. Siméon Lourdel M.Afr., early White Fathers missionary
