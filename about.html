Our History

St. Mary's College Kisubi (SMACK) was founded in 1906 by Fr. Raux Modeste of the Missionaries of Africa (White Fathers). Initially established at Rubaga, the school relocated to its current location in Kisubi in 1924. SMACK is owned by the Catholic Archdiocese of Kampala and has grown to become one of Uganda's most prestigious secondary schools.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide quality holistic education rooted in Catholic values, nurturing young men to become responsible citizens and leaders.

Vision

To be a leading center of academic excellence and moral formation in East Africa.
